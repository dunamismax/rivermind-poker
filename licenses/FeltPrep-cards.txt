The deck (web/src/lib/art/cards/, drawn by web/scripts/art/deck.js)

RiverMind Poker's look (Phase 5C, decision D-18) draws its playing cards
after the code-drawn cards of FeltPrep, a separate project by the same owner,
first drawn there at FeltPrep commit cd8293a (2026-11-26, its step P3.1):
web/src/lib/ui/PlayingCard.svelte, SuitPip.svelte and cards.ts. RiverMind takes
FeltPrep's card layout (a large rank at the top left, a small pip under it and
a large pip at the bottom right), its four suit colours (clubs green, diamonds
blue, hearts red, spades near black) on its warm white face and edge, its four
suit shapes, and its burgundy lattice back. RiverMind redraws them on its own
71 x 95 card box, draws each rank as strokes rather than text, and adds navy
and green backs drawn the same way.
