# RiverMind Poker design

The site has one look (D-18): a dark card room drawn in CSS and SVG, with charcoal and slate surfaces, a deep muted felt inside a dark rail, one gold accent for the viewer's seat and the turn, the system's own fonts, soft shadows and smooth motion. The aim is the easiest poker table to read and play on a phone or a desktop. It is dark only for 1.0 (Q-12). The [design reference page](../web/src/routes/style/+page.svelte) (`/style` on any build) shows every piece of the kit as the site draws it.

Nothing is an image file but the deck, the sprites and the icons, which are SVG drawn by code (and two PNG icons rasterised from the same shapes). The interface art is original and licensed with the rest of the project under [GPL-3.0-or-later](../LICENSE); the deck's provenance is under [The deck](#the-deck).

## The rules

- **Tokens, not values.** Every colour, text size and animation is a theme token in [`theme.css`](../web/src/styles/theme.css); the markup names them by role (`bg-felt`, `text-gold`), never by value.
- **Cards snap.** Cards ask for a size with `--k` on any parent (1 is 71 CSS pixels wide) and snap to the nearest whole number of *device* pixels per card unit, using `--dpr` (set in `app.css` from `resolution` media queries) and CSS `round()`, so a card's edges and a fan's overlap fall on whole device pixels on 1x, 2x, 2.625x and 3x screens alike.
- **Readable first.** A card's rank is drawn at 2 or more device pixels per card unit wherever it must be read (`--card-min: 2` keeps the cards a phone pod shows legible). Every suit has its own colour, so a card never depends on telling red from black. Amounts are shown in full, with the gold chip for stacks.
- **Nothing loops by moving.** An infinite animation changes colour, opacity or a shadow, never a box's position: the seat to act glows in place (`turn`), since a smooth bob never stands still and moved a name or a menu from under a finger. `prefers-reduced-motion` stops every animation.
- **No inline style or script.** The site's Content-Security-Policy forbids both, so positions are classes (`seat-places.css`, generated by `layout.ts`), swatches are SVG fills, and animations are CSS keyframes, never Svelte transitions.

## Styling: Tailwind on the kit's own theme

The pages are styled with Tailwind CSS 4 utilities (P4B.2). The theme, [`web/src/styles/theme.css`](../web/src/styles/theme.css), replaces Tailwind's own entirely (`--*: initial`), so the utilities offer only what the kit has:

| Token | Utilities | Values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--color-<name>` | `bg-felt`, `text-gold`, `border-deep-slate`, `fill-ink`, `accent-gold` | The palette's colours, by name with hyphens. No other colour has a class. |
| `--spacing` | `p-3`, `gap-4`, `mt-1`, `w-88`, `min-h-24`, `size-7` | One step, 2px: the number counts steps, so `p-3` is 6px. Whole numbers only. A length that is not a whole step is written out (`gap-[3px]`), which marks it as off the grid. |
| `--text-<size>` | `text-xs`, `text-s`, `text-m`, `text-l`, `text-xl` | 11, 13, 16, 22 and 28px. |
| `--font-sans`, `--font-mono` | `font-sans` (the default family), `font-mono` | The system's own fonts. |
| `--text-shadow-ink`, `--text-shadow-title` | `text-shadow-ink`, `text-shadow-title` | A soft shadow under light text on art, and under the site's name. |
| `--animate-<name>` | `animate-blink`, `animate-wait`, `animate-drop`, `animate-slide`, `animate-pop`, `animate-fade`, `animate-rise`, `animate-nudge`, `animate-speak` | Eased, never stepped ([Tokens](#tokens)). |

[`app.css`](../web/src/app.css) imports Tailwind and the theme, puts the kit (`kit.css` and `sprites.css`) in the `components` layer so a utility always overrides a kit class, and keeps the page's own element styles in `base`, over Tailwind's preflight. It also defines:

- **Breakpoints** as variants with the layouts' exact queries: `lt-800:`, `lt-640:`, `lt-600:`, `ge-600:`, `lt-460:`, `lt-360:` for the pages, and for the table `lap:` (1279px and narrower: the narrower side column), `tall:` (899px and narrower, or 1279px and narrower held upright: one column), `phone:` (639px and narrower, or 1099px and narrower held upright: pods and the tall table), `tab:` (640 to 1099px held upright, adjusting `phone:`) and `pocket:` (639px and narrower: a phone alone, which has no site header at a table). Later ones win, in that order.
- **`hover:`** on every device, as the kit's buttons do; Tailwind's default skips touch screens.
- **Sources.** Only the routes, `lib/nl` and the three shared components are scanned, and a few ordinary words (`table`, `filter`, `transform` and others) are ruled out as classes, because Tailwind reads every word of a source as a candidate.

What is not a utility, and why:

- **The kit** (`kit.css`, `sprites.css`): frames, buttons, fields, the slider and the table are CSS rules, and sprites are `background-image` rules over the generated SVG.
- **Card geometry** (`PlayingCard.svelte`, and the hand in `NlSeat.svelte`): the `--k`, `--dpr` and `round()` snapping, the fan's overlap in snapped card units, and the `deal` keyframes that use them.
- **Seat positions** (`seat-places.css`): generated from `layout.ts`, one class per seat count and place.
- **Fixed values with meaning** stay as written: translucent ink is `bg-[rgb(12_13_21/0.94)]` rather than `bg-ink/94`, because Tailwind's opacity modifier rounds the alpha differently, and box shadows are `[box-shadow:…]`, which keeps Tailwind's ring and shadow machinery out of the page's CSS.

A class that only names an element (`seat`, `meta`, `result`) is kept where the tests or the seat's card rules select by it. Do not name one after a utility: `grow` on a label once made it grow.

Check any change to styles or markup with `pnpm visual` against captures of the commit before it ([CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md#comparing-screenshots-p4b2)).

## Tokens

Each palette name has a role: gold is the accent, felt is the table, `green-light` a win, `red-light` a loss, ink the darkest ground and paper the main text. `warn` is the turn clock under 30% and the voice meter's middle. The deck's colours (P5C.3) are tokens of their own: `card` (`#fffdf7`) with its `card-edge`, the three backs' colours, and one colour per suit, FeltPrep's: clubs green `#19703a`, diamonds blue `#1a58ad`, hearts red `#b4231b`, spades near black `#1a1d22`.

| --- | --- |

## The kit

[`web/src/styles/kit.css`](../web/src/styles/kit.css) holds the shared classes, drawn without an image file, and the sizes of the sprites and of the compact hand's window, which layouts depend on:

| Class | Use and drawing |
| --- | --- |
| `.ui-panel`, `.ui-raised`, `.ui-inset`, `.ui-paper` | Frames: a panel (deep-slate with a faint edge and a soft shadow), a raised panel (slate), a sunken ink well (logs, inputs), and paper with ink text. Each keeps a transparent 6px border under its surface, with the edge drawn as a ring just outside it. |
| `.ui-gold`, `.ui-glow`, `.ui-danger` | The viewer's seat (a 2px gold ring), the seat to act (a gold-light ring with a soft gold halo), an error (a red ring on red-deep). |
| `.ui-btn` with `.red`, `.blue`, `.amber`, `.green`, `.gold` | A rounded face with a 1px edge and a small shadow, the label in semibold and centred. Fold is red, check and call blue, bet and raise amber, a chosen option gold. Slate is the default. Pressing darkens the face and sinks it with an inner shadow; the label never moves. Disabled is deep-slate with a haze label. `.small` for secondary actions. A link drawn as a button has no underline. |
| `.ui-btn.choice` | A choice that turns gold with an ink label when chosen: a pressed toggle (`aria-pressed`), or the face of a checked radio button or checkbox before it. |
| `.ui-table` | A deep felt lit from above (a radial gradient) inside a dark rail, its rail and corner counted in `--table-unit` (3px, or 2px on smaller screens). |
| `.ui-field`, `select.ui-field`, `.ui-range` | An ink well with a dusk edge; a select's caret is a chevron of two gradients; the bet slider is a slim rounded groove and a round gold thumb as large as a chip. |
| `.sprite` with `.chip-*`, `.avatar-*`, `.pot-pile`, `.dealer-button`, `.mini-cards`, `.emblem`, `.voice-*` | Sprites as background images ([`sprites.css`](../web/src/styles/sprites.css)), sized in spacing steps. |
| `.card-peek` | A compact hand: two cards fanned tight in a 74 × 50 card-unit window (the first card's index, a ten's included, and the second card's corner), at 2 device pixels per card unit or more. Lists of hands and small seats. |

`PlayingCard` draws a face, a back (`card={null}`, optionally `back="lattice-navy"`) or an empty board slot (`card={undefined}`), sized by the nearest `--k` and snapped as above, smooth, with a soft shadow and an eased deal. Faces have alt text such as “Jack of hearts”. The connection lamp is round, the table list's seat markers are dots, and where the board's places overlap, each empty slot's edge is cut out of the card before it, so the row reads as single edges. To leave a phone's header room for the player's name, the site's name drops “Poker” below 460 pixels wide.

## How the art is made

The deck, the sprites and the icons are drawn by code in [`web/scripts/art/`](../web/scripts/art/), from the palette in `theme.css`, and `pnpm art` writes them: the deck into [`web/src/lib/art/cards/`](../web/src/lib/art/cards/), the sprites into [`web/src/lib/art/ui/`](../web/src/lib/art/ui/) and the icons into `web/static/`. Vite gives each file a content-hashed URL. From `web/`:

```sh
pnpm art                      # write the art
pnpm art --preview /tmp/art   # also write art.html: the deck and sprites on the felt and on the page, at --k 0.75 to 2
```

Open the preview at each device-pixel ratio before accepting a change. Never edit a generated file by hand. [`art.spec.ts`](../web/src/lib/art/art.spec.ts) fails if a file drifts from its generator or a directory holds another, if a file is not well-formed SVG of drawing alone (no script, style or external reference), if a colour is off the palette, if a face does not show its own rank and suit, if the ten comes within 3 units of the edge, if a back changes under a half turn, if a sprite class has no sprite or a sprite is not the kit's size, or if an icon drifts, its shapes are not the header mark's, or `app.html` names other icons or another theme colour.

**Sprites** (P5C.4). Each is a hand-drawn SVG at the size `kit.css` gives it, in CSS pixels. The chips are a face, a rim and eight stripes; the pot is three stacks seen from the side; the dealer button's D is a stroke; people and bots sit in a round slate badge, a person as head, hair and shoulders and a bot as a robot in its kind's colour (the page still tags it “Bot”, D-7); the RiverMind mark is a gold spade with a river across it; the mini cards are two burgundy backs fanned; and the voice glyphs are paper strokes inside an ink outline, so they read on their ink tag at 11 and 22 pixels. No letter in them is text.

**The iPhone app** (P5D.5). `pnpm art` also writes the app's look, from the same sources, with [`swift.js`](../web/scripts/art/swift.js): the tokens of `theme.css` and the kit's colours in `kit.css` into `Tokens.swift`, every card and sprite into `ArtLibrary.swift` (each SVG the generators draw, read back into its shapes, which the app draws natively), and the app's asset catalog: its icon at 1024 in iOS's default, dark and tinted styles, the launch screen's night and the gold accent. [docs/ios.md](ios.md#the-look-p5d5) has how the app draws them and how they are checked against the site.

**The icons** (P5C.4, P5C.7). The favicon, its PNG fallback and the home-screen icon are the mark, gold on a night square: an SVG favicon (`static/favicon.svg`, 32 × 32 with rounded corners), a 48 × 48 PNG of it for browsers that take no SVG icon, and a 180 × 180 PNG for the home screen, whose square is left unrounded because the phone rounds it. `theme-color` is night, `#11141b`. [`icons.js`](../web/scripts/art/icons.js) renders the PNGs from the same shapes as the SVG with a small rasteriser of its own (paths flattened to lines, 8 × 8 samples a pixel), so `pnpm art` writes the same bytes on any machine.

## The deck

The cards (P5C.3) are the code-drawn cards of the owner's FeltPrep project, redrawn on RiverMind's 71 × 95 card box: a large rank at the top left, a small pip under it and a large pip at the bottom right, in the four suit colours above on warm white with a thin edge, with FeltPrep's own pip shapes. FeltPrep sets the rank in the system's sans-serif; an SVG shown as an image takes whatever font the device has, so here each rank is drawn as round strokes instead, the same on every device, and the ten always keeps its two digits clear of the edge. The backs are FeltPrep's burgundy lattice and the same lattice in navy and green (`lattice-burgundy`, the default, `lattice-navy`, `lattice-green`), each the same after a half turn, and the empty board slot is a card's outline sunk into the felt. [`deck.js`](../web/scripts/art/deck.js) draws them, and [`card-assets.ts`](../web/src/lib/card-assets.ts) maps cards to files, lists the backs and preloads only the faces and the back in use. The drawings' provenance is in [`licenses/FeltPrep-cards.txt`](../licenses/FeltPrep-cards.txt): FeltPrep is AGPL-3.0 with its artwork reserved, and the owner, who holds both projects, contributes these drawings to RiverMind under GPL-3.0-or-later (D-18).

## The table

[`NlTable.svelte`](../web/src/lib/nl/NlTable.svelte) lays out three screens:

- **Wide (landscape, 900px and up).** The table on the left; the action dock, the seat tools and the hand log in a column on the right (360px from 1280px, 300px below). Opponents’ cards ask for `--k: 1`, the viewer’s and the board’s for `--k: 1.4` (1 below 1280px); on a 2x screen that is 2 and 3 device pixels per card unit. The felt is 21:10 (18:10 below 1280px) and, on a screen too narrow for that shape to be tall enough, as tall as the screen leaves it up to 420px (400px), so a full ring keeps clear of the board at 1024 × 768 and 1280 × 720. A seat is `--seat-w` wide (176 to 190px with the window, 150px below 1280px) and never changes size: it keeps room for its cards whether or not it holds any, another seat keeps a row for its tags, and the viewer’s tags and clock hang under the seat, so a plate stays where it is when a hand is dealt or folded and when the turn comes round. A bot’s seat leaves the site’s name out (“Easy 4”, “Calling bot 2”), since nine seats cut off at “RiverMind Eas…” cannot be told apart; the whole name is in the seat’s title and label and in the hand log. Another seat’s turn clock is a bar along the lower edge of its plate.
- **Tall (phones, and tablets held upright).** The game is the first screen, and everything else is below it. The bar, the table and a zone as tall as the action panel add up to the small viewport (`100svh`, what the browser leaves with its toolbars showing), so on arriving nothing is cut off and nothing needs scrolling, and the table never changes size. A phone leaves the site header out at a table; its bar carries the way back to the lobby, the hand, the blinds and the connection lamp. To keep the bar to one line in voice at a private table, a phone's bar has 4px gaps and its blinds no chip. The dock is pinned to the bottom of the screen: while waiting it is a strip, with the seat tools above it at the top of the zone, and on the viewer’s turn the action panel rises over the zone and stops at the table’s edge. The hand log and the play-money note follow below. Opponents are compact pods with a small card-back badge (two backs in miniature): one row across the top, a pod each side below the board, and at eight and nine seats a pod on each bottom corner of the rail beside the viewer, so a 9-seat table fits a felt 356px tall and never covers the board. A bot’s pod shows the part of its name that tells it apart (“Easy 4”, under its “Bot” tag). Bets sit under a pod in the top row, beside one at the side and just above one on a bottom corner. Cards a pod shows sit by it in a 74 × 50 card-unit window, fanned tight so the first card’s index (a ten’s included) and the second card’s corner show, at 2 or more device pixels per card unit; upright tablets show the whole cards. The viewer keeps a full seat with `--k: 1` cards (1.4 on tablets).
- **Narrow landscape** (below 900px) uses the single column with the wide table, the seat tools above it. A phone held sideways gets this layout and does not fit it: the dock covers the table.

Nothing moves under a finger when the turn’s action panel opens. On the wide screens the dock keeps its height; on the tall ones the panel covers the seat tools without moving them, and its presets and slider, not Fold, Call or Raise, are what lands where they were. Seats wear their avatar (a robot for every bot, labelled “Bot”, per D-7), name, stack and a turn clock, one smooth rounded bar (green, warn when under 30%, red in the time bank). Bets sit between each seat and the pot with a chip whose color follows the bet’s size in big blinds. On the wide table a bet keeps clear of its own seat by pixels, because a seat is as large on a small felt as on a large one (`betSpot` in `layout.ts`, with the distances in `--seat-drop`, `--tag-level`, `--tag-side`, `--seat-clear`, `--board-above` and `--board-below` on the stage): under the tags of a seat on the top rail, beside them for the seat straight across, which has the pot under it, a seat’s half width away from a seat at the side, and just over or under the board’s row where the seat is level with it. The action panel lights one bet size at a time: where two come to the same amount (the minimum raise is often half the pot), the one picked, or else the first. A menu that opens from the bar (the invite link, a player’s menu, table voice) lies over the table at every size, so opening one never moves the table or a seat under the pointer.
